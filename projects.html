Mars compositional clay stratigraphies: spectral analysis (IDL/Python)

PhD research · Purdue University · 2022–present

I developed a spectral analysis pipeline for the first two chapters of my PhD, investigating clay deposits and weathering on ancient Mars using CRISM hyperspectral observations.

  • Maps clay absorption-band centers and depths, detects clay-bearing pixels and classifies clay phases, calculates spectral parameters, extracts spectra from automated Regions of Interest.
  • Focused on the mapping and validation of kaolinite, smectites, iron oxides, and acid sulfates.
  • First-author publication in Geology, follow-up spectral database paper currently in review.

IDL · Python · ENVI · CRISM · Spectral parameter mapping

Perseverance rover: Mastcam-Z

Student collaborator · 2022–present

I co-lead the Geologic Context and Multispectral working groups and develop science campaigns for the Perseverance rover. I've delivered more than 70 presentations to the mission science team and am trained in Mastcam-Z downlink and uplink operations.

My multispectral analyses of igneous and sedimentary materials in Jezero crater focus on mineral composition, fluvial deposits and aqueous alteration.

Multispectral imaging · Mineral identification · Geological interpretation

Volcanic and tectonic mapping on Venus

MSci research · Imperial College London · 2021–2022

Mapped volcanic and tectonic features in Atla Regio using Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) to investigate the evolution of Ozza and Maat Montes. This work formed my master's thesis and contributed to a study of plume volcanism on Venus.

Remote geological mapping · Volcanology · Structural interpretation

Eastern Kalpin fold-and-thrust belt

Independent mapping project · Imperial College London

A remote mapping study employing Google Earth, WorldView, and spectral data from Landsat and ASTER to unravel the tectonostratigraphic evolution of the eastern Kalpin fold-and-thrust belt in Xinjiang, China. Awarded the E. Ambrosiadou Prize for top academic performance in the geological mapping project.

Geological mapping · Stratigraphy · Structural geology
